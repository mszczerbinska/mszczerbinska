# Hi, I'm Maria 👋

I'm a bioinformatics MSc student at the University of Warsaw, 
currently figuring out how gut bacteria interact with the food 
we eat — and what that means for our health.

My background is a bit of a mix: I started with biotechnology 
(BSc at UG&MUG in Gdańsk), got pulled into the computational 
side of things, and now I spend my time somewhere between 
the lab bench and the terminal.

---

## What I'm up to right now

🧬 **MSc thesis @ [OLAB, IIMCB Warsaw](https://olab.com.pl)**  
Investigating the genetic basis of azo food dye reduction by gut 
bacteria. I isolate bacterial strains, run high-throughput 
degradation assays, and then try to explain the phenotypic 
variability using comparative genomics and variant analysis.  
*(Kraken2 · Nextflow · HMMER · Snippy · SnpEff · ONT sequencing)*

💊 **Summer intern @ AstraZeneca Warsaw** *(Regulatory Affairs, Oncology)*  
Getting a look at how science translates into medicine from 
the regulatory side — which fits nicely with my BSc work on 
signalling in luminal breast cancer.
